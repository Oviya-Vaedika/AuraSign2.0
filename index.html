Aura Sign is an educational prototype exploring accessible communication interfaces.

Text to motion

Enter a phrase and explore an animated, illustrative avatar with playback controls and a gloss tracker.

Camera lab

Preview your camera and inspect hand landmarks as a foundation for future gesture-recognition work.

Honest by design

Sign languages have their own grammar and regional variation. This prototype does not claim to replace fluent signers or interpreters.
