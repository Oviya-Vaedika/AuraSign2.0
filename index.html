Aura Sign is an educational prototype exploring accessible communication interfaces, with a focus on future ISL learning tools.

01

Text to motion

Enter text and explore illustrative avatar movement, playback controls, and a token tracker.

02

Camera lab

Preview your camera and inspect hand landmarks as a starting point for future gesture-recognition research.

Honest by design

ISL has its own linguistic structure and community-specific usage. This prototype is not a substitute for fluent signers or qualified interpreters.
